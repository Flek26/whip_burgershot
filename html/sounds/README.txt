Put the kitchen's sounds here (Config.Sounds in config.lua). .ogg or .mp3; a missing one is simply silent.

  sizzle.ogg   a short loop of a hotplate sizzling   (played while a grill has food on it)
  fryer.ogg    a short loop of a deep fryer bubbling (played while a fryer has food in it)
  pour.ogg     a soda being poured                    (the drinks machine)
  wrap.ogg     paper being wrapped                    (the prep counter)

Free ones (CC0) can be found on freesound.org. Change the names in Config.Sounds if yours differ.
